      AI and disaster response

      Technology That Helps Save Lives.

      AI for Flood Prediction

      Problem: Floods can escalate rapidly when rainfall, river levels, and storm patterns change together.

      Use: Machine learning analyses rainfall, river gauges, weather models, and historical floods to support early warning and evacuation planning.

      Benefits: Faster alerts and better preparation.

      Limitations: Forecasts are uncertain and need local expert validation.

      Human role: Disaster management teams interpret warnings and guide evacuations.

      AI for Wildfire Detection

      Problem: Forest fires spread quickly and can be hard to detect early.

      Use: Satellite imagery, drones, and computer vision monitor hotspots and fire fronts.

      Benefits: Faster detection and better situational awareness.

      Limitations: Weather, smoke, and poor image quality can reduce accuracy.

      Human role: Fire crews verify risks and manage response in the field.

      AI for Earthquake Risk Assessment

      Problem: Earthquakes are difficult to predict with precise timing and location.

      Use: AI analyses seismic patterns, fault activity, and structural risk information.

      Benefits: Improved monitoring, risk mapping, and post-event assessment.

      Limitations: AI cannot reliably predict the exact time and place of an earthquake.

      Human role: Seismologists and local agencies combine data with field judgment.

      AI for Disaster Damage Assessment

      Problem: Access to damaged zones may be limited after a disaster.

      Use: Computer vision and satellite images help identify damaged buildings, roads, and flooded areas.

      Benefits: Faster reporting of infrastructure damage and priority needs.

      Limitations: Image quality and missing ground truth can lead to errors.

      Human role: Responders validate findings on the ground.

      AI for Rescue Operations

      Problem: Responders need to move fast while prioritising areas based on urgent need.

      Use: Optimisation models help identify accessible routes, allocate vehicles, and prioritise search zones.

      Benefits: Better resource deployment and faster response.

      Limitations: Models rely on incomplete or changing field conditions.

      Human role: Command centres and rescue teams make final decisions.

      AI for Relief Distribution

      Problem: Delivering food, water, medicine, and shelter must match urgent needs.

      Use: AI-assisted logistics helps connect demand, inventory, and route planning.

      Benefits: More efficient aid delivery and reduced waste.

      Limitations: Data gaps and inequities can exclude vulnerable communities.

      Human role: Aid agencies and local leaders ensure fair and humane distribution.

      Responsible Limits

      • AI predictions are never perfectly accurate and should never be treated as absolute truth.
      • Poor-quality or incomplete data can produce misleading results.
      • Technology cannot replace trained responders, medical teams, and local authorities.
      • Human oversight is essential in every step of disaster management.
      • Digital systems must be designed to include vulnerable communities and avoid exclusion.
