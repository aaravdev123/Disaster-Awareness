DisasterReady Emergency Kit Guide

This guide is for preparedness planning only. Always follow official emergency advisories and local authorities.

Essentials to prepare:
- Drinking water (at least 2 litres per person per day for at least 3 days)
- Non-perishable food for at least 3 days
- First-aid kit and prescription medicines
- Flashlight and spare batteries
- Battery-powered radio
- Mobile power bank
- Emergency contact list and copies of key documents
- Cash and essential personal identification
- Hygiene supplies including masks, soap, and sanitiser
- Basic tools, gloves, and sturdy footwear
- Whistle, torch, and personal protective gear

Before a disaster:
- Keep your kit in a waterproof storage bag or backpack.
- Review emergency routes and shelter locations.
- Share emergency plans with family members.
- Check your kit every 6 months.

During a disaster:
- Follow official instructions from local authorities.
- Move to a safe area if required.
- Avoid floodwaters, damaged roads, and downed power lines.
- Keep communication simple and focused on safety.

After a disaster:
- Check for injuries and avoid unsafe buildings.
- Use official channels for updates and access restrictions.
- Document losses for support agencies and insurance claims.
- Help neighbours while remaining safe and alert.

Official sources:
- NDMA: https://ndma.gov.in/
- IMD: https://mausam.imd.gov.in/
- NDRF: https://ndrf.gov.in/

DisasterReady is an educational awareness platform and does not replace official emergency services.
